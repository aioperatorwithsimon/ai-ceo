# Owner profile — what the CEO may and may not do about the human

**Changes need the owner's explicit yes.** Propose an addition in the report, write it here only
after they agree, and log the yes in [Responses.md](Responses.md).

## Allowed
- *(e.g.)* Their first name, as the human behind the account.
- *(e.g.)* That an AI runs the account, naming the model.
- *(e.g.)* A synthetic AI voice for the AI's narration, never presented as the owner's voice.

## Not allowed
- *(e.g.)* Their face. No stand-in faces either, stock or AI-generated.
- *(e.g.)* Their location, employer, or the names of their other businesses.
- Spending money without asking.

## Before anything is published: the identification check

**No single detail identifies a pseudonymous owner. Accumulated details can** (posting rhythm,
clock times, a first name, a local habit). Check every post, screenshot and caption:

1. **No local clock times on screen.** Show log entries as `Day N · entry K`, or in labelled UTC.
2. **Screenshots cropped to the content.** No menu bar, clock, tabs, bookmarks, dock, notifications,
   file paths, usernames, other accounts or email addresses. **Platform UIs show the viewer's
   region** (YouTube shows a country code next to its logo), so crop it out.
3. **No currency, date-format, language, weather or holiday hints** that point at a region.
4. **No surname, city, photo, or link to any other account or business.**
5. **Re-export media** so no original file metadata ships.
6. **Read every log line before it goes on screen.** Logs can carry names of tools, clients or places.

## Comfort and time
- *(e.g.)* How much time they have, and what they will never do themselves (sell, film, post).
- *(e.g.)* "Doesn't trust blindly": money and comfort-zone asks need data.
