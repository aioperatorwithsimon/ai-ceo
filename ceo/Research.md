# Research — what we know about the market, with sources

**External facts the CEO decides on.** Every entry has a date and a source. Findings expire as
platforms change, so **re-check anything older than ~3 months before betting on it**, and rewrite
the entry when it's re-checked. Your own performance data doesn't go here; keep it with your per-post data.

Labels: **[V]** verified against the source · **[S]** secondary or vendor claim · **[I]** inference.

## The "AI runs this account" angle *(researched 2026-10-13)*

- **[V] Earlier "AI runs X" experiments grew on real decisions, real stakes and visible failures,
  not on success.**
  - HustleGPT (X, March 2023) went from about 3k to 100k+ followers in roughly 10 days. Its business
    made about $130, and it was criticised for overclaiming
    ([mercury.com](https://mercury.com/blog/ai-bootstrapping-jackson-fall),
    [futurism](https://futurism.com/startup-run-chatgpt)).
  - Anthropic's Project Vend (Dec 2025): the coverage centred on the agent being manipulated and
    making mistakes ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-vend-2)).
  - Others: Truth Terminal (crypto-driven), Andon Market's "Luna" (a physical store, 2026).
- **[I] No short-form "AI is CEO of this account, Day N" series was found.** TikTok search couldn't
  be crawled, so absence isn't proof.
- **[I] The counter alone is a weak hook.** A decision with a consequence ("the AI deleted 3 posts
  today") is a strong one. Viewer votes act like Twitch Plays Pokémon / HustleGPT's "investors".

## Formats and retention *(2026-10-13)*

- **[V] Voice beats music-only on retention.** In brand Facebook Reels, speech in the first 3s gave
  +11.7% retention at 3s and +24.7% at 10s. **Text overlays hurt speech-led reels** (−6% at 10s)
  and helped music-led ones. About 62% of brand reels have no speech
  ([Emplifi, 19 Mar 2026](https://emplifi.io/resources/facebook-reels-data/)).
- **[V] All three platforms down-rank unoriginal content in 2026.**
  - Instagram stops recommending mostly-reposted content (Apr 2026,
    [petapixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)).
  - YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule (Jul 2025) demonetises mass-produced, repetitive videos.
  - TikTok's For You feed excludes "unoriginal or reused material without anything new"
    (effective 24 Sep 2026).
  - **[I] Templated text-on-stock is the most exposed format. Real screen evidence is original by
    definition.**
- **[S] Series turn viral spikes into followers.** One build-in-public creator reached ~30k in 3-4
  months, with 4 videos driving half of it. He replied to ~90% of comments
  ([startupspells](https://startupspells.com/p/build-in-public-playbook-rajan-chida-0-to-370k-followers-125-days)).
  ⚠️ He shows his face, so this isn't evidence for faceless growth.

## Platform rules on AI and automation *(2026-10-13)*

- **[V] Automated posting is against all four platforms' terms.**
  - TikTok: "strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts…"
    ([guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)).
  - YouTube's ToS forbids access "using any automated means".
  - Instagram bars automated access without permission.
- **[V] Realistic AI audio should be disclosed on Meta and TikTok.**
  - Meta's AI-disclosure rule covers "realistic-sounding audio … digitally created"
    ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/manipulated-media/)).
  - YouTube requires disclosure mainly for realistic depictions of real people or events
    ([Google](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)).
  - **We disclose in every caption.**
- **[V] Instagram limits recommendations for *undisclosed* AI-person profiles. Labelled ones
  reportedly see no reach change** (Aug 2026,
  [MediaPost](https://mediapost.com/publications/article/417580/instagram-limits-reach-for-creator-profiles-withou.html)).
  **[S]** No solid source shows a correct AI label cutting reach.

## Reputation, privacy and the relaunch *(2026-10-13)*

- **[V] Sharing small numbers in public is established practice.**
  - Buffer has published salaries and metrics since 2013
    ([buffer.com](https://buffer.com/resources/salary-system/)).
  - The downsides (looking like bragging, copycats, giving away know-how) show up at large revenue,
    around $20-30k/month
    ([coryzue.com](https://coryzue.com/writing/building-in-private),
    [bootstrapped founder](https://thebootstrappedfounder.com/the-increasing-risk-of-building-in-public/)).
- **[V] Disclosing AI use costs a little trust. Being caught hiding it costs more**
  (Schilke & Reimann 2025, 13 experiments,
  [arizona.edu](https://news.arizona.edu/news/disclosing-ai-use-can-backfire-research-shows)).
  71% of US adults want AI labels.
- **[V] Timezone can be inferred from activity patterns** (OSINT,
  [PhishLabs](https://phishlabs.com/blog/using-social-media-osint-to-determine-actor-locations)).
  **[I] No single detail identifies a pseudonymous owner, but they accumulate.** That's why
  [OwnerProfile.md](OwnerProfile.md) has the identification check.
- **[S] Instagram's advice is to pivot on the existing account, not start a new one**
  ([summary](https://influencermarketinghub.com/adam-mosseri-on-pivoting-your-instagram-content/)).
  **[I]** At 455 followers almost all growth comes from non-followers, so a pivot costs little.

## Money at small scale *(2026-10-13)*

- **[V/S] Platform payouts are close to nothing below 10k followers.**
  - TikTok Creator Rewards: needs 10k followers, ~$0.40-1.00 per 1k qualified views.
  - YouTube Shorts ad revenue: needs 1k subs + 10M Shorts views per year
    ([Google](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240)).
- **[S] Newsletter: about $100-$2,000+/month at 1,000 subscribers** (beehiiv's own guide). No
  reliable data exists on digital-product income under 10k followers.
- **[I] Likely order:** email list → sell the real system as a product → affiliates and a paid
  community at 10k-50k.

## What the fast-growing faceless accounts do *(researched 2026-10-13, ~55 sources)*

- **[S] Faceless is the harder road.** Across 2,130 founder accounts: median followers 26,200 with a
  face vs 1,108 without, median views 2,951 vs 387
  ([JOLT](https://joltexclamationpoint.com/research/face-vs-faceless)). **[I] Faceless accounts that
  win do it on story, participation or a character, not on production.**
- **[V] The fastest faceless growth on record was a serial, participatory AI show.** "Fruit Love
  Island" reached 3.3M TikTok followers in 10 days: numbered daily episodes, cliffhangers, viewers
  voting through a form, comments read on air. It was later mass-reported and ended
  ([NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/viral/ai-fruit-love-island-tiktok-slop-popularity-rcna264946)).
  **Our vote mechanic is the same lever.**
- **[S] Hooks that name another person plus a conflict outperform hooks about the product by
  50-250×** in one same-niche comparison: "My landlord said… so I showed her what AI thinks" got
  234K views; "See your room in 12+ styles" got ~1-2K
  ([substack](https://gameplaydev.substack.com/p/how-his-openclaw-agent-larry-got)). Other hooks
  that work: second person ("POV: you…"), starting mid-action, a blunt character line.
- **[V] AI-voiced TikToks get lower engagement, and less so when the voice is the creator's
  established identity** (554,252 videos,
  [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4676705)). **[I] Keep one consistent AI
  voice that IS the character. The owner's real voice works as a rare cameo.**
- **[V] Viral reach converts badly.** An IG trial reel at 1.3-1.4M views brought 130 follows, about
  0.1 per 1k ([Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/instagram-trial-reels-study-what-we-learned-after-a-trial-reel-hit-1-million-views)).
  **So follows per 1k views FALLS when a post breaks out.** That's why the kill rule also tracks
  followers per day.
- **[V] An honest scale check:** Claude Plays Pokémon reached only ~15k Twitch followers despite
  Anthropic's backing. Long, slow autonomous content doesn't grow by itself
  ([TwitchTracker](https://twitchtracker.com/claudeplayspokemon)). Each episode needs one decision,
  a consequence, and a payoff inside the clip.
- **[S] Length (2026, brand datasets):**
  - Instagram: 45-60s had the best engagement and median views
    ([Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)).
  - TikTok: median views rise up to 120-180s. Creator Rewards needs ≥1 min.
  - Shorts: 50-60s averaged the most views.
  - **[I] Our own old feed read was that 50s+ died, so start at 35-50s.**

## How each platform distributes in 2026 *(researched 2026-10-13, ~50 sources)*

- **[V] Instagram's top signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**; sends weigh
  more for non-followers (Mosseri, Jan 2025,
  [SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)).
  Other rules:
  - The first 3 seconds are the hook.
  - **Text shouldn't cover most of the screen**
    ([Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)).
  - **Max 5 hashtags.**
  - **Trial Reels need 200+ followers** on a professional account.
  - "Link a Reel" chains episodes together.
- **[V] TikTok's For You uses watch/skip, likes, shares and comments, plus content info that
  includes "the country in which the content was published"**
  ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7655285288050104852)).
    location.
  - TikTok's AI label "won't affect distribution".
  - Buffer, 11.4M posts: more posts raise the ceiling, not the typical post
    ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/)).
- **[V] YouTube counts a view from the first frame** (since Aug 2026), so judge on **engaged views**
  and viewed-vs-swiped ([YouTube](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/)).
  Since Oct 2026 Shorts favour original content. The "inauthentic / templated content" rule hits
  **monetisation**, not reach, **so vary the template.**
- **[V] Facebook Reels predicts watch time and completion.** Its inputs include "similarity to other
  reels" and the author ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/)).
  A view counts when playback starts, so **200-330 views with zero engagement is probably
  scroll-past autoplay, not watching.**
- **[S/I] Timing.** Post when the target audience is awake. It matters less than quality, but it
  decides who the first test-pool viewers are.
- **[V/S] Cadence.** No platform says volume hurts. Our own data favours 3/day; similarity between
  reels is a Facebook input. **Vary hook, visual and length inside the series.**
- **[V] There's no "inactivity penalty."** Check Instagram Account Status and TikTok's per-video
  eligibility notices instead of guessing about shadowbans.

## Production craft *(researched 2026-10-13, ~60 sources)*

- **[S] Burned-in, word-synced captions are the safest bet** for sound-off viewing. ~69% say they
  watch muted in public (Verizon/Publicis survey). The uplift size is unproven. A 2024 eye-tracking
  study found sound-off viewers rely heavily on subtitles
  ([PLoS One](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11458047/)). → built: `captions.py`.
- **[S] Animate screenshots with *targeted* push-ins and highlights**, and change something on
  screen every 2-4s. Put the decision in the first 2s. Open a loop early and close it at the end.
  End so the video loops back to its start.
- **[V] ElevenLabs `eleven_v4` launched on 2026-09-28** and is now recommended. It has no speed
  slider, uses tags sparingly, and its behaviour with premade voices isn't documented
  ([ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/eleven-v4)).
  Picked v4 by ear (more natural than v2). Measured pace: v4 149 wpm vs v2 187 wpm, so
  we apply 1.1× atempo → 164 wpm.
- **[S] Music 18-20 dB under the voice** (WCAG suggests 20). Platforms publish no loudness spec;
  −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP is the consensus.
- **[S] Covers:** the IG grid crops 9:16 to 3:4, so keep "DAY N" plus the decision inside the
  centred 1080×1350. A consistent cover reads as a series.

## Audience, other channels, amplification *(researched 2026-10-13, ~70 sources)*

- **[V/S] The audience is big and growing.** Claude Code weekly actives doubled in early 2026
  ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-raises-30-billion-series-g-funding-380-billion-post-money-valuation)).
  r/ClaudeAI ~1.17M, r/AI_Agents ~451K, r/ClaudeCode ~436K (tracker figures). In r/ClaudeAI,
  **stories and lessons outscore how-tos by orders of magnitude.**
- **[V] Agent experiments get press and Hacker News traction from their failures** (Project Vend:
  279 / 197 points). HN bans AI-written text and automated posting, so any HN post is the owner's,
  human-written. Generic "my Claude setup" posts score 2-16; a strong angle scores 450-750.
- **[V] Open-sourced Claude Code setups are the biggest growth assets in this niche**
  (obra/superpowers ~299k stars, everything-claude-code ~278k; read live via the GitHub API). One
  agent operator's top earner was a setup guide (~$41k, secondary source). **[I] A setup that runs real operations rather than code is differentiated.**
- **[S] Threads has an official posting API** and passed X in daily mobile users (Jan 2026). An AI
  posting the daily ledger there costs ~0 of the owner's time after setup. **X forbids browser-scripted
  posting (risk of permanent suspension); its API is allowed and has an "automated" label**
  ([X](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation)).
- **[V] Never cold-contact people as the AI.** AI Village's agent emailed Rob Pike unsolicited and
  it blew up ([Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Dec/26/slop-acts-of-kindness/)). No cold
  DMs, no emails, no mass replies.
- **[V] Novelty alone fades.** AI Village raised far less in its second year ("the novelty of an
  AI-run fundraiser is a bit diminished",
  [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/QopBrHKDCgi5DPtMX/more-capable-ai-less-money-raised)).
  **Participation and real stakes have to carry it.**
