# Strategy — the current bet

**Owned by the CEO, current state only.** When the bet changes, rewrite this file and record the
change, with its evidence, in the [ledger](ledger/). *(Below is the live bet of the account this
repo comes from, as a worked example. Replace it with yours.)*

## Where it started (2026-10-13)

88 posts of text-on-stock-footage reels: ~56,000 views and 455 followers across Instagram, TikTok,
YouTube and Facebook. **About 8 follows per 1,000 views.** The content got watched and gave nobody
a reason to follow.

## The bet: the experiment is the content

> **"An AI was handed this account. Every decision and every mistake, in public. Goal: 10,000."**

- **A reason to follow:** a series with stakes is an open loop.
- **True and verifiable:** real screenshots, real numbers, a real ledger. Original by definition,
  which is what all three big platforms' 2026 originality rules reward.
- **The honest headwind:** faceless accounts grow far slower than face-fronted ones, and novelty
  alone fades. **The edge has to be story + participation + a consistent character.**

## The episode

**One decision, its consequence, and a payoff, in 35-50 seconds. Every episode works on its own**
(most viewers are seeing you for the first time).

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0-2s | The consequence, in ≤12 words, over the real screenshot of the result. No preamble |
| 2-8s | The stakes / open loop |
| 8-15s | One line of series context, never as the opener |
| 15-30s | Evidence: real numbers, what was weighed, the option rejected; something changes every 2-4s |
| 30-40s | Payoff that closes the loop, with no wrap-up phrasing |
| end | A send prompt: "send this to someone who…" |

- **Shares spread content; comments don't.** Instagram ranks on sends per reach, and practitioners'
  data (a 5,400-Short study, faceless app accounts) found little link between comments and reach. A
  viewer vote is a story device, used when the decision is interesting and always honoured in public,
  not the default call to action.
- **Hooks:** the consequence, a person plus a conflict ("the owner said no"), or blunt AI honesty about
  a failure. Each must promise desire, fear or curiosity.
- **One consistent synthetic voice**, disclosed, plus word-synced captions.
- **Write an expected score (1-10) before posting** and judge against it, not against the last episode.
- **When a format works, repeat it across topics.**

## Cadence and timing

- 1 episode a day while quality settles, then 2-3. Space posts ≥4h apart.
- Post when the target audience is awake (for an English-speaking US/UK audience, US morning).

## How it's measured, and what kills it

- **Primary:** followers per channel, read live. **Also:** followers gained per day (7-day average),
  follows per 1,000 views, and what the comments say.
- **Kill rule:** after 10 episodes, if follows per 1,000 views AND followers per day are both below
  double the old baseline, the bet is wrong. Say so plainly and change it. (Breakouts lower follows
  per view, so never judge on that number alone.)

## Income — after the audience exists

Nothing gets built to sell until there's an audience to sell to. **Money is the only honest
signal:** one module + a pre-sale + real money. Dozens pre-pay → build it. Two → stop.
