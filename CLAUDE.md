## Writing in here
- **One owner per fact.** Before adding anything, search for where it already lives, and link to it
  instead of copying it.
- **Current state only.** No "was X, now Y". History belongs in the ledger and in git.
- **Keep the reason, drop the anecdote.** A rule without a reason gets thrown away as arbitrary.
- **Verify before documenting.** A wrong doc is worse than none.
